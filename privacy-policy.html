Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Jason Townsley Counselling & Therapy Services (“we”, “us”, or “Jason Townsley”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website or contact us through it. It does not describe the separate clinical records governed by your therapeutic relationship, which are handled under applicable health-information law and professional standards.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form or email us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any information you choose to share in your message. We may also collect standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, through analytics tools, in aggregated and non-identifying form.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a meet & greet or appointment.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and this website.
  • To comply with legal and professional obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Website enquiries are directed to a private, monitored email account.

Please don’t send sensitive health details

Email and web forms are not fully secure. We ask that you avoid including detailed personal health information in your initial message. A brief note about your reason for reaching out is all that’s needed.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use cookies and third-party analytics (such as website hosting and mapping services) to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may then work differently.

Third-party links

Our website may link to external sites (for example, an online booking tool, a map provider, or crisis resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (including PIPEDA and Ontario’s PHIPA where applicable), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at Connect@Arterie.ca to make a request.

Data retention & security

We keep website enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and to meet legal or professional record-keeping requirements, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email Connect@Arterie.ca or call 289-442-5060.
