Not medical or psychological advice

Nothing on this Site should be taken as medical, psychological, or professional advice for your specific situation. Reading this Site does not establish a therapist–client relationship. Please consult a qualified professional before making decisions about your mental health.

Individual results vary

Therapy outcomes differ from person to person. No specific result is promised or guaranteed. Descriptions of services reflect a general approach, not a prediction for any individual.

Crisis & emergencies

This Site does not provide crisis or emergency services and is not monitored around the clock. In an emergency, call 911. If you are struggling with thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) or call Talk Suicide Canada at 1-833-456-4566.

Professional registration

Jason Townsley is a Master’s-level Registered Social Worker (MSW, RSW). Services are delivered in accordance with the standards of the Ontario College of Social Workers and Social Service Workers (OCSWSSW).
