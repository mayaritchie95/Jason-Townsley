Sessions & formats

A rhythm that fits your life

Every journey starts with an intake, then continues with sessions sized to your needs. (I don’t currently offer couples therapy.)

Intake — Youth (13–18)

First appointment

A thorough, welcoming first session to understand what your teen is facing and shape a plan that fits them. Parents are involved in the way that’s most helpful.

Intake — Adults (19+)

First appointment

We map out what brought you here, what you’re hoping for, and how we’ll get there together — the foundation for everything that follows.

Individual session

50 minutes · the standard hour

The most common format: enough time to go deep on what matters while keeping steady, sustainable momentum week to week.

Flexible lengths

25, 50 or 80 minutes

Shorter check-ins or longer, deeper sessions — we choose the length that suits the work and your schedule.

Parent / Guardian only

Support for the adults in a youth’s life

A chance to discuss parenting approaches, share important information and perspective, and leave feeling more confident in your at-home guidance for your youth — without your teen present.

Family session

Multiple family members present

When it helps to have more than one family member in the room, we can address shared struggles together and work through emotional blocks as a family.

Clinical Supervision

For clinicians

Support for fellow clinicians in conceptualizing and navigating difficult cases, working through countertransference, or finding a space to help reduce burnout.

Circle of Care

For medical & mental-health professionals

Consultation for professionals within the Circle of Care on shared clients. To refer your patient, contact Jason to arrange the transfer.

Therapeutic approaches

Modalities I draw on

Every person is different, so I tailor my approach to you. I’m trained across several evidence-based modalities — including a specialized credential as a DBT Skills therapist — and often integrate more than one to fit your goals.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Recognizing and reshaping the thought patterns and behaviours that keep you stuck.

DBT

Dialectical Behavioural Therapy

Practical skills for managing intense emotions, distress, and relationships — offered as a certified DBT Skills therapist.

IFS

Internal Family Systems

Understanding and healing the different ‘parts’ of yourself with curiosity and compassion.

MBSR

Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction

Using mindfulness to reduce stress and stay grounded in the present moment.

EFT

Emotion-Focused Therapy

Working with emotions as a guide toward insight, connection, and change.

Attachment

Attachment Therapy

Exploring how early relationships shape the way you connect today — and healing those bonds.

Trauma

Trauma Therapy

Gentle, paced work to process traumatic experiences and restore a sense of safety.
