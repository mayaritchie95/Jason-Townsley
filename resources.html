When you need support right now

These resources are here for you around the clock. They don’t replace therapy, and I’m not a crisis service — but in an urgent moment, please reach out to one of these first.

  • Emergency: 911
  • Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 988
  • Talk Suicide Canada: 1-833-456-4566
  • Kids Help Phone: 1-800-668-6868
  • ConnexOntario: 1-866-531-2600
