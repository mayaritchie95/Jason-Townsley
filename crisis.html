Crisis support

If you’re in crisis,
help is available now

You don’t have to get through this moment alone. Please reach out to one of the services below.

For imminent or serious harm

Go to your nearest hospital emergency room, or call 911 for emergency services.

Please know: I’m not equipped to be a crisis-response service. My practice can’t provide urgent or after-hours emergency care. When you’re in a mental-health crisis, the services below are staffed to help right away.

After you’ve reached safety

If you’re my client and you’ve had to access crisis supports, please let me know when you’re able to. Together we can assess what happened, process it, and better prepare ourselves in case a crisis happens again.
