Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Just ask — I’m happy to help.

Is the meet & greet really free?

Yes. The meet & greet is a complimentary, no-obligation consult call so we can both decide whether we’re a good fit before you commit to anything.

How much do sessions cost?

Fees depend on session length (25, 50, or 80 minutes) and whether it’s an intake or a follow-up. I’ll share current rates during your free meet & greet so there are no surprises.

Are your services covered by insurance?

As a Registered Social Worker (RSW), my services are covered by many extended health benefit plans. Coverage varies by provider, so it’s worth checking your specific plan for ‘Registered Social Worker’ coverage. I provide receipts for reimbursement.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes. I offer secure video sessions to clients anywhere in Ontario, as well as in-person appointments at my Burlington clinic.

What ages do you work with?

I work with youth aged 13 and older, and adults. I don’t currently offer couples therapy.

How do I pay?

Payment details are arranged when you book. I’ll walk you through everything clearly before your first session.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for reasonable notice if you need to cancel or reschedule so the time can be offered to someone else. I’ll go over the specifics when we begin working together.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share is private and protected within the limits of the law and my professional obligations as an RSW. I’ll explain the few legal exceptions (such as risk of serious harm) at the outset.

Have a question I didn’t cover?

Reach out any time — or book a free meet & greet and ask me directly.
