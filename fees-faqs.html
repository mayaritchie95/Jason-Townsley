Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Just ask — I’m happy to help.

Is the meet & greet really free?

Yes — completely free, with no obligation. Research consistently shows that the best therapy starts when there’s a good-enough fit between client and therapist. These brief meet & greets are meant to get us started on the right foot, so we can both sense whether we’re the right match before you commit to anything.

How much do sessions cost?

Current fees are listed right on my online booking page — please take a look there for the most up-to-date rates. View fees & book →

Are your services covered by insurance?

As a Registered Social Worker (RSW), my services are covered by many extended health benefit plans. Coverage varies by provider, so it’s worth checking your specific plan for ‘Registered Social Worker’ coverage. You’ll receive an email receipt you can submit for reimbursement.

How do I pay?

When you create your profile, the Jane platform securely holds your credit card on file. It automatically completes payment and emails you a receipt by the end of the day. If applicable, you then submit that receipt to your insurance provider for reimbursement.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can easily change or cancel your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Once inside that 24-hour window, the fee for a missed appointment or late cancellation is the full session amount. That said, life happens to all of us — so everyone is offered one free “Grace” cancellation per calendar year.

How does the cancellation list work?

If you don’t see a soon-enough date available, you can add your name to the cancellation list with your preferred dates and times. When a cancellation opens a spot, clients on the list get first notification — the spot is held for you for 2 hours before it’s released to the general public. Join the cancellation list →

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes. I offer secure video sessions to clients anywhere in Ontario, as well as in-person appointments at my Burlington clinic.

What ages do you work with?

I work with youth aged 13 and older, and adults. I don’t currently offer couples therapy.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share is confidential — protected by law, by professional ethics, and, most importantly, by the safe therapeutic atmosphere that makes excellent therapy possible. There are a few exceptions to confidentiality, which Jason will explain during your intake appointment and any time you’re curious.

Have a question I didn’t cover?

Reach out any time — or book a free meet & greet and ask me directly.
