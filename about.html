Hi, I’m Jason

A steady presence in a hard season

I’m a Master’s-level Registered Social Worker providing mental-health and psychotherapy treatment for youth (13+) and adults. I’ve been doing this work since 2007 — close to twenty years of sitting with people through the hardest and most hopeful chapters of their lives.

Being a male therapist is, for many of my clients, part of why they chose me. There are far fewer of us in this field, and I know how much it can matter — for a teen, for a partner, for anyone who’s felt they’d be more comfortable talking things through with a man who gets it.

My style is calm and approachable. I want the room to feel like a safe haven — never cold or clinical — while still being a place where real change happens.

How I work

Two things, held together

The safety to heal

I’ll never rush you

Trauma, grief, and childhood attachment wounds need a steady, patient space before anything else. We move at a pace your nervous system can trust.

The honesty to change

And I won’t let you stay stuck

When you’re ready, I’ll gently challenge the behaviours and mindsets that aren’t serving you — because insight is only the beginning; living differently is the goal.
