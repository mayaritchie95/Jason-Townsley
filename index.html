Meet Jason

I’m a Master’s-level Registered Social Worker who has spent nearly two decades in the therapy world — since 2007. I work with youth (13+) and adults, and I bring something to the room that many clients tell me they were specifically looking for: the steady presence of a male therapist.

My approach holds two things at once. I’ll meet you with the warmth and safety needed to heal from trauma and attachment wounds — and, when you’re ready, I’ll gently challenge the patterns and mindsets that are keeping you stuck.
